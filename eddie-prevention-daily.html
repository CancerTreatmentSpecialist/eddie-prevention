🚭 Tobacco & nicotine

Did you smoke, vape, or use any nicotine today?
Every tobacco-free day is a fresh start. Free quit coaching: call 1-800-QUIT-NOW or visit smokefree.gov. Ask your doctor about nicotine replacement and quit medicines, which can double your chances of quitting.
Quitting tobacco at any age lowers your risk of cancer, heart disease, stroke, and lung disease. Vapes contain nicotine, which is addictive.
Source: CDC: Smoking and Tobacco Use
If you drink heavily every day, talk with your doctor before stopping suddenly — alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous. Free, confidential help 24/7: SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357.

🌊 Cravings

Any cravings for nicotine or alcohol today?
Cravings usually pass in 5–10 minutes. Try a short walk, a glass of water, slow deep breaths, or texting a friend. Logging them helps you spot your triggers.
