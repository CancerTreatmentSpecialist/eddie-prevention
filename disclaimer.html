Education-Only Disclaimer

Effective date: October 2026

For education and self-tracking only

Eddie Prevention provides general health information and tools to track your own habits. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Using Eddie Prevention does not create a doctor-patient relationship.

Talk with your doctor

Always seek the advice of your doctor or another qualified health professional with questions about your health, and before starting, stopping, or changing any medicine, diet, exercise, or habit. Never ignore professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you read in Eddie Prevention.

Stopping alcohol safely

If you drink heavily every day, talk with a doctor before you stop. Stopping suddenly can cause dangerous withdrawal, including seizures.

About the targets shown

Daily and weekly targets in Eddie Prevention are based on general national guidelines for adults, including the U.S. Physical Activity Guidelines, the Dietary Guidelines for Americans, CDC sleep and BMI guidance, and American Heart Association blood pressure categories. They may not be right for you, especially if you are pregnant, have a health condition, or take medicines.

Emergencies

Eddie Prevention is not for emergencies. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

1-800-QUIT-NOW — free coaching to quit smoking or vaping
