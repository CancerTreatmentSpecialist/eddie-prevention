Why I created Eddie Prevention

After decades as a surgical oncologist, I've seen how many cancers — along with heart disease and diabetes — trace back to everyday habits. Prevention isn't one big decision. It's small choices, made daily. Eddie Prevention is built to help everyone make those choices count. — Derrick J. Beech, MD, FACS · Board-Certified Surgical Oncologist
