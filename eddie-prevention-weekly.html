A reading above 180/120 can be dangerous. Wait a few minutes and check again. If it's still this high, or you have chest pain, shortness of breath, weakness, or trouble speaking, call 911.
Healthy blood pressure is below 120/80. High blood pressure usually has no symptoms but raises the risk of heart attack and stroke. Less salt, more activity, less alcohol, and not smoking all help.
